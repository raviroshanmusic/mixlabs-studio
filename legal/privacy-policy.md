- We collect what an account needs to work: your name, email, and what you do on

- **Your footage is yours.** We store it so the product works. We don't licence
  it, train on it, or look at it except when you ask us to fix something.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information** — name, email address, and optionally company and role,
provided by you or by whoever invited you. Passwords are hashed by our
authentication provider; we never see them.

**Project content** — everything you or your collaborators upload: video and
audio drafts, editorial turnovers, reference documents, deliverables, and the
comments, timecodes, milestones and approvals recorded against them.

**Usage records** — which projects you belong to, your role on each, and a
record of actions such as uploading a draft or resolving a note, so the project
history is accurate.

**Technical data** — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps)
kept for security and troubleshooting.

**Billing information** — if you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment processor
handles your card details. **We never receive or store card numbers.**

**We do not collect** location data, biometric data, advertising identifiers, or
special-category data as defined by the GDPR. We do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 18.

## 3. Who else touches it

We use a small number of infrastructure providers. Each is bound by contract to
process data only on our instructions. A current list, with what each one
handles and where, is published at `/legal/subprocessors` and updated when it
changes.

We share personal information with no one else — not advertisers, not data
brokers, not analytics companies. We disclose data to authorities only where
legally compelled, and we will tell you unless prohibited from doing so.

## 4. Where your data lives

MixLabs is operated from India. Our infrastructure providers store data in the
**United States**, so using the service involves an international transfer of
your data.

Where the GDPR applies, these transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.
Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies, transfers are made
in accordance with its cross-border provisions.

If your organisation requires data residency in a specific region, contact us
before subscribing — we would rather tell you no than surprise you later.

---

## 5. How long we keep it

**Project content** stays until you delete it or close your account. When an
account is closed we delete content within **30 days**, except where we're
required to keep records for longer.

**Account records** are deleted within 30 days of closure.

**Billing records** are kept as long as tax law requires, typically eight years
in India.

**Server logs** are kept for 90 days.

Deleted files are removed from our storage. Encrypted backups may persist for up
to 30 days before rotating out.

---

## 6. Security

- Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
- Access is enforced at the database level, per project and per role — not
  merely hidden in the interface.
- Media is served through short-lived signed links, not public URLs.
- Staff access to customer content is restricted and only occurs in response to
  a support request or a specific security investigation.

No system is perfectly secure, and we don't claim otherwise. If a breach affects
your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required —
within 72 hours where the GDPR or DPDP Act applies.

Email **[PRIVACY EMAIL]**. We respond within 30 days — and within 7 days for
erasure requests where the DPDP Act applies. We will not charge you for this or

**California residents:** we do not sell or share personal information as those

months. You retain the rights to know, delete, correct, and to non-discrimination.

**EU/UK residents:** you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

**Indian residents:** you may nominate another person to exercise your rights
should you be unable to, and you may complain to the Data Protection Board of
India.
