# Subprocessors

**Last updated [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

These are the third parties that process customer data on our behalf. Each is
bound by contract to process data only on our instructions.

Publishing this list is a GDPR and DPDP requirement, and enterprise customers
will ask for it during procurement. Keep it accurate — an out-of-date
subprocessor list is a compliance failure on its own.

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Data handled | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Backblaze B2** | Media and file storage | All uploaded content — drafts, turnovers, documents, deliverables | United States (`us-east-005`) |
| **Supabase** | Database and authentication | Account details, project metadata, comments, permissions | United States |
| **Vercel** | Application hosting | Request logs, IP addresses | United States / global edge |
| **Resend** | Transactional email | Names, email addresses, notification contents | United States |
| **[PAYMENT PROCESSOR]** | Subscription billing | Billing contact, payment details (card data never reaches us) | [REGION] |

## Notes

**No analytics or advertising subprocessors.** We run none, so none appear here.

**We will give at least 30 days' notice** before adding a subprocessor that
handles customer content, so customers with approval requirements can object.
